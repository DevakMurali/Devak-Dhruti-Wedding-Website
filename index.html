FAQs

Good Questions

What is the RSVP deadline?

Please RSVP by October 7th. This is very important to ensure we have seating, food, and other accommodations prepared for your attendance.

Why isn't the RSVP form loading?

The RSVP form uses Google Forms. Make sure you are logged into your Google Account on the browser to ensure the RSVP form loads properly.

Can I RSVP on behalf of others?

Yes! When you enter your name, you will be provided with RSVP options for yourself and your direct friends/family members. However, once an RSVP has been submitted for an individual, you can no longer edit it on their behalf — the individual must enter their own name to make any changes.

Will food be served?

Yes, food will be served at various points throughout the day. If you are attending the wedding ceremony, lunch will be served following the ceremony. Prior to the reception, there will be a cocktail hour with light bites and non-alcoholic drinks. Dinner will be served during the reception.

What kind of food will be served?

The wedding will be catered by Rangoli Restaurant, serving primarily North/South Indian and Indo-Chinese cuisine. If you have any food allergies or sensitivities, please contact the hosting families to let them know.

What is the dress code?

Festive Indian attire is encouraged for the morning ceremony. Kindly refrain from wearing red and white combinations. We would also appreciate it if you could try to match our color palette! For the evening reception, formal Western or Indian attire is welcome.

Where is parking available?

Yes, there are plenty of free parking spots available throughout the venue. Please visit this link for more details.

What time should guests arrive?

Please visit the Events page for timing details for the event(s) you will be attending. We recommend arriving 20–30 minutes early to allow ample time to find parking and get settled inside the Event Center. Kindly refrain from arriving late to avoid disruptions to any of the programs.

Can guests take pictures at the wedding?

You are welcome to take photos, but please refrain from using flash or taking extended video recordings. We ask that you be mindful of keeping phones out of sightlines to avoid blocking other guests' views or appearing in our professional photos. Rest assured, professional photos and videos will be shared with everyone after the wedding!

Who should I contact for additional questions?

Please contact Dhruti Ramani and/or Devak Murali for any additional questions.
