Location & Travel

Hanover, Maryland

The Venue

Maryland Live! Casino & Hotel

All of our wedding celebrations will take place at Maryland Live! Casino & Hotel in Hanover, Maryland — a premier event destination in the heart of the Arundel Mills corridor, situated just 15 minutes from Baltimore and 30 minutes from Washington, D.C.

All events are held within the Event Center at Maryland Live! Casino & Hotel — a dedicated venue space with its own entrance, completely separate from the casino floor.

Flying In?

By Air

We recommend flying into Baltimore/Washington International Thurgood Marshall Airport (BWI) — it's only 8 miles from the Event Center, roughly a 15-minute drive, and rideshare is readily available curbside.

If BWI doesn't work for your travel plans, Reagan National (DCA) is approximately 35 miles south and Dulles (IAD) is about 45 miles west — both are easy drives on I-95 or the Beltway.

On-Site Parking

Where to Park

For the most convenient arrival, park in the surface lot directly adjacent to the Event Center — your best bet for a short, straightforward walk in. It may look like a Costco lot, and that's because Costco shares the Arundel Mills complex, but this area is owned and operated by Maryland Live! Casino & Hotel and is reserved for Event Center guests. You'll see Maryland Live! Casino & Hotel signage once you're in it.

A large multi-level covered garage is also available on property. Depending on where you enter, you may need to walk through part of the building to reach the Event Center — just ask any staff member for directions and they'll be glad to help.

🅿️

All parking on site is complimentary for Event Center guests.

Navigation Tip

Finding the Entrance

Most navigation apps will route you to one of Maryland Live! Casino & Hotel's many entrances — but for the Event Center specifically, you'll want to use these coordinates directly in your maps app:

Event Center Entrance Coordinates 39.15675, -76.72833 Open in Google Maps

If you do end up at the wrong entrance, any staff member on site will happily point you in the right direction.
