FAQs

Good Questions

What is the dress code?

Festive Indian wear is encouraged for the morning ceremony. For the evening reception, formal Western or Indian attire is welcome. When in doubt — more color is always a good idea!

Is parking available?

Yes, complimentary parking is available at Maryland Live! Casino. Rideshare drop-off is also convenient right at the entrance.

Are children welcome?

We love your little ones! Children are welcome to join us for the full celebration.

Will there be vegetarian options?

Absolutely. Both the lunch and reception dinner will include a full vegetarian spread alongside non-vegetarian options.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

We kindly ask that guests put phones and cameras away during the ceremony so everyone can be fully present. Our photographers will capture every moment — we'll share the full gallery after!
