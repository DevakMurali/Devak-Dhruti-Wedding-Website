The Wedding Day

Hanover, Maryland

hover an event to read more
7:45 – 8:30 AM

Guest Arrival

Guests arrive dressed in vibrant traditional attire — sarees, lehengas, sherwanis, and kurtas — and are warmly welcomed by the hosts. The venue fills with the fragrance of flowers, the sounds of festive music, and the joy of family and friends reuniting to celebrate and bless the couple's union.

8:30 – 8:45 AM

Baarat

The Baarat is the groom's festive wedding procession, where he arrives surrounded by his family and friends dancing jubilantly to the beat of the dhol. Energy runs high and no two Baarats are ever the same — expect the unexpected! It's a high-energy, celebratory parade that sets an electric, festive tone for the entire wedding celebration!

8:45 – 9:00 AM

Pokhna

The Pokhna is the heartwarming moment where the bride's family formally welcomes the groom upon his arrival at the wedding venue. The bride's mother performs an aarti, offers sweets, and blesses the groom as he is received with love and open arms. It's a beautiful and touching ritual that officially marks the beginning of two families becoming one!

9:45 – 11:45 AM

Wedding Ceremony

The Wedding Ceremony is the sacred heart of the celebration, where the couple comes together before family, friends, and the sacred fire to exchange vows and be united as one. It's a deeply moving and joyous occasion that marks the beautiful beginning of a lifelong partnership!

11:30 AM – 1:00 PM

Lunch & Photos

After the ceremony, guests are treated to a wonderful feast as everyone comes together to eat, laugh, and celebrate the newly married couple! It's also the perfect opportunity to capture lasting memories — gather your family and friends and don't miss your chance to get a photo with the newlyweds.

5:30 – 6:30 PM

Cocktail Hour

Cocktail hour is the perfect time to unwind, mingle, and keep the celebration going! Guests enjoy handcrafted drinks and delicious bites while connecting with old friends and making new ones. Think of it as the ultimate warm-up for the reception — the energy is just getting started!

6:30 – 11:00 PM

Reception

The Reception is the grand finale of the day — a spectacular celebration of the newly married couple featuring dinner, dancing, and unforgettable moments! Heartfelt toasts, incredible music, and a packed dance floor make for an electric atmosphere as family and friends come together to send the newlyweds off in style.
